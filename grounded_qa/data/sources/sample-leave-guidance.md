# Eligibility

An employee at Example Co. may request planned leave after 90 days of continuous service. Requests are made
in writing to the employee's manager at least 14 days before the leave begins.

# Duration

Planned leave is limited to 12 weeks in any rolling 12-month period. Leave may be taken in blocks of no less
than one week unless the manager approves a shorter block.

# Pay during leave

Planned leave is unpaid. An employee may apply accrued vacation time to any part of the leave.

# Returning to work

An employee who returns within the 12-week limit is placed in the same position or one with the same pay,
benefits and schedule.
